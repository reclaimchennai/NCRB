Chennai: traffic accidents and suicides over time

What changed over time in NCRB's figures on traffic accidents and suicides, for Chennai, Tamil Nadu and India. Each series follows one table across every year it was printed, oldest first; scanned years (before about 2000) were read with OCR and an AI document model, and a year is shown only when its figures add up to the total printed with them.

Built by analysis/run.py from the NCRB tables in this repository. Data: NCRB, Accidental Deaths & Suicides in India (ADSI).

Chennai road accidents by time of day

NCRB's city tables count accidents, not deaths, by time of day; Chennai (Madras until 1996) is printed every year from 1995. Left out because their figures do not add up to the printed total or disagree with the month table: 1995, 2000.

The busiest three hours were 18-21 h in 12 of 28 years (in 2024: 09-12 h). The evening and night (18–06 h) held 42% of road accidents in 2001 and 40% in 2024. Use the buttons to switch between counts and each year's share of the day, and the menu to pick out one year.

Road accidents peaked at 9,845 in 2011 and were 3,762 in 2024. 2018 and 2020 put an unusually large count in 00–03 h; that is how NCRB printed them (the same figures appear in the state/city table and the full report).

Chennai road accidents by month

Month tables for Chennai cover 2000–2024. Over these years the month with the largest average share was Mar (9.3%) and the smallest Nov (7.4%); an even spread would be 8.3% a month. April 2020 (lockdown) held 1.9% of that year.

Road deaths by time of day (Tamil Nadu)

Deaths by time of day and month are printed only state-wise, and only from 2021; for Chennai NCRB publishes the number of accidents and, separately, yearly totals of persons killed. In Tamil Nadu 26% of road deaths in 2024 happened between 18 and 21 h.

Suicides in Tamil Nadu by means

Hanging was 23% of suicides in 2004 and 60% in 2024; poisoning (insecticides and other poison together) went from 40% to 33%. NCRB's wording of the means changes between editions; they are mapped to one list (analysis/harmonise.py) and the printed label is kept in the CSV. The oldest means table that adds up is 1972's: poison: other 45%, other / not known 22%, drowning 18%, hanging 11% (read from the scan with the AI model; not drawn, being far from the unbroken run).

Suicides in Tamil Nadu by profession

Profession tables run 2004–2024. In 2014 NCRB split 'others' into new groups, so shares before and after 2014 are not comparable for the 'other' professions. Daily wage earners, counted from 2014, were 24% of suicides in 2014 and 53% in 2024. House wives were 18.9% in 2004 and 14.1% in 2024. Students were 4.4% in 2005 and 6.4% in 2024. Unemployed people were 15.2% in 2004 and 2.8% in 2024.

Profession by sex and age is printed state-wise only from 2021 (the third chart).

Suicides by sex and age group

Sex and age by state is printed for 2004–2015, 2021–2024 (from 2021 taken from the means-by-age table's total row); for cities, including Chennai, for 2004–2015. Between 2016 and 2020 NCRB printed age and sex only for all India. Tamil Nadu: men were 62% of suicides in 2004 and 74% in 2024. Chennai: men were 55% of suicides in 2004 and 71% in 2013.

Suicide rates: States, UTs and cities

Rates are printed for 1976, 1988–1992, 2004–2024 (1993–2003 are not in the downloadable tables; the scanned years before 1993 are kept only where suicides ÷ population matches the printed rate). In 2024 Tamil Nadu's rate was 25.9 against 12.2 for India, 6th of 36 States/UTs.

Chennai's rate was 31.0 in 2021 and 17.5 in 2024. City rates use a fixed census population (Chennai 64.3 lakh to 2010, 87.0 lakh from 2011), so a change in the rate is a change in the count of suicides; Chennai's count fell from 2,699 in 2021 to 1,581 in 2022.

Major cities by sex and age group

NCRB printed suicides by sex and age for each of the 35 (from 2011, 53) big cities in 2004–2015, and in the scanned 1989–1990 reports; it has not printed them since. The request for a 2019 distribution in major cities by sex and age with ICD-10 codes S00–T98 matches the Registrar General's Medical Certification of Cause of Death report, not NCRB, so it is not in this dataset.
