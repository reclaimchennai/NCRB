          How reliable is each year?

          Wherever NCRB prints a State, UT, city or all-India total, the rows above it are added up and compared. Each dot is one year: the share of those totals that match. Text PDFs and spreadsheets (since about 2000) are copied digit for digit; older volumes are scanned books read by OCR.
